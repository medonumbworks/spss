        About

        Data sources

        • Prices, market caps and history: CoinGecko, with Coinpaprika as a fallback
        • Fear & Greed index: alternative.me
        • Social buzz: public Telegram channels, Reddit and crypto news feeds
        • Derivatives positioning: Binance and Bybit public endpoints
        • Prediction markets: Polymarket

        Update cadence

        A GitHub Actions job rebuilds this dashboard every hour. The header shows how old the data you are looking at is; when it is more than two hours old the Telegram tab also tries a live refresh from the sentiment feed.

        Limitations

        • Information only, not financial advice. The forecasts are probabilities, not recommendations, and short-term crypto moves are mostly noise.
        • Everything is built on free, keyless APIs with strict rate limits, so numbers can lag or be missing for a while.
        • A coin whose typical move over the horizon is under about 1 % (roughly what buying and selling costs) gets no up/down call, only "Moves too little".
        • Trending coins that Bitpanda does not list are shown for information only; most small speculative coins lose most of their value.
        • Pump warnings and risk scores are heuristics, not guarantees either way.
        • The app never links to a place to buy any coin.
